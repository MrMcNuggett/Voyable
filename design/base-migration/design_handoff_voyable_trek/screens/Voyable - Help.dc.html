Creating your first trip

Getting started · 3 min read

A trip holds everything for one journey: your day plan, places, transports, bookings, packing lists, and costs.

Start a trip

From your dashboard, select New trip and give it a name. Dates are optional — you can add them later once your plan takes shape.

Add places

Use the places sidebar inside the planner to search for a destination or drop a pin. Drag any place onto a day to schedule it.

  • Search finds places by name or address
  • Imported lists bring places in from a file
  • Unplanned places stay in the sidebar until you assign them to a day

Invite your travelers

Share gives collaborators edit access to the whole trip, or a read-only link for people who just need the itinerary.
